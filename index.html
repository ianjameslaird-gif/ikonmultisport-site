FAQ

What does Ikonmultisport offer?

Ikonmultisport supplies golf simulators, mobile sports simulators, LED backlit fabric lightboxes and event supply chain services for events, exhibitions and venues in Singapore.

Can the sports simulator come to my event?

Yes. Singapore's first mobile sports simulator, with a football simulator plus 100+ sports, is delivered and set up at your venue, from hotel ballrooms to offices and outdoor activations.

Do you have golf simulators for events?

Yes. Indoor golf simulators can be set up for corporate events, product launches, roadshows and venues, with realistic courses and challenges for guests.

What lightbox sizes are available?

There are 17 standard frame sizes from 1x1m up to 9x3m, used for trade show booths, brand backdrops, counters and towers. Lightboxes can be rented or bought.

What is event supply chain?

Sourcing, supply and logistics of event production equipment, fabric graphics and displays, so your event materials arrive ready to install.
